A cloud drive, kept in your own Telegram.

TeleDrive backs up and organises your files in a private channel on your Telegram account. There is no TeleDrive server, no account to create with us, and encryption is one switch away.

What a stranger would see.

With encryption on, someone reading your channel without your key learns the file count, the sizes and the timestamps. Nothing else.

The recovery passphrase has no reset path. That is the point, so set a hint when you create it and keep the passphrase out of the hint.

On device
The app and Telegram databases are sealed with a random key wrapped by the platform: Android Keystore, Windows DPAPI, or a local key file on Linux and macOS. Thumbnails and caches are encrypted by default.
In Telegram
Only sealed bytes, random file names, encrypted captions and an encrypted folder tree.
Key custody
Content keys are random, wrapped by the platform master key, and never leave the device unwrapped.
Recovery
The content key is backed up to your channel, sealed with your passphrase (PBKDF2, 310k iterations). Restore it on a new device and everything unlocks.

      Running in four steps.

      You need a Telegram account and a pair of API credentials, which Telegram hands out for free.

      1. Get your API credentials

        Sign in at my.telegram.org, open API development tools and create an app. Any name works. Keep the api_id and api_hash it shows.

      2. Enter them in TeleDrive

        They are stored encrypted on the device, never logged, and only ever sent to Telegram. Because they are yours, nobody else's rate limit can touch you.

      3. Sign in

        Use your phone number and login code, or a QR code. With Telegram on the same device, one tap on Confirm in Telegram does it.

      4. Choose what to back up

        Pick folders, and turn on Encrypt uploads with a recovery passphrase if you want the channel sealed.

      Before you install.

      Does Telegram limit how much I can store?

      Telegram does not cap the total size of a channel. Single messages have a size cap, and TeleDrive works around it by storing a large file as several messages. Opened in the Telegram app, such a file shows as parts; only TeleDrive puts it back together.

      Can Telegram read my files?

      Without encryption, your files sit in your private channel like any other upload. With Encrypt uploads on, the channel only holds sealed bytes, random names, encrypted captions and an encrypted folder tree.

      What happens if I lose my phone?

      Install TeleDrive on another device, sign in, enter your recovery passphrase and the whole drive is back, folders and all.

      Why do I need my own API ID?

      Shared credentials pool everyone's usage together, so one heavy user can get the whole app rate limited. Your own pair keeps your traffic separate, and it is never bundled into the app or sent anywhere except Telegram.

      Can I stream without downloading first?

      Yes. Video and audio play as they arrive and you can seek freely. Streaming checks the frames it plays; a full download verifies every byte, so use that when integrity matters more than starting fast.

      Is TeleDrive made by Telegram?

      No. It is an independent open source project built on TDLib, Telegram's official client library, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.
